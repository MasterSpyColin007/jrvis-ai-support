How do I restore my JRVIS Plus purchase?

Open JRVIS AI → Settings → Subscriptions → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel JRVIS Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select JRVIS AI and tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Plus access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and request a refund for JRVIS Plus.

JRVIS isn't responding / something looks broken. What should I try?
  1. Force-quit JRVIS AI and reopen it.
  2. Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Check that you have an internet connection (cloud features need one).

Still stuck? Email us with what happened and what you tried.

How do I delete my data?

Most of your data (memories, settings) can be deleted inside the app. To request deletion of anything stored with your account on our servers, email pratercolin2010@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
