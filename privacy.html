Privacy Policy — JRVIS AI

Last updated: September 20, 2026

JRVIS AI ("the app") is developed by Colin Prater. Your privacy matters: the app is designed so that as much as possible happens on your device, and data you create stays yours.

What the app collects

  • On-device data: conversations, memories, settings, and preferences are stored on your device and, where you enable it, synced through your own iCloud account. We do not have access to your iCloud data.
  • Cloud AI requests: when you use cloud-powered features (chat, voice, web search, image generation), your prompt and relevant context are sent to the AI provider fulfilling that request so it can respond. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.
  • Account & diagnostics: if you contact support or submit feedback, we receive the information you include (such as your email address and device details). Crash and usage diagnostics may be collected through Apple and Firebase services to fix bugs and improve the app.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your conversations for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as needed to operate the app (for example, AI providers fulfilling your requests, Apple's App Store for subscriptions, and cloud infrastructure providers).

Subscriptions

JRVIS Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive subscription status (active, expired, billing period) from Apple's systems but never see your payment details.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data can be deleted at any time from within the app. To request deletion of any data associated with your account on our servers, email pratercolin2010@gmail.com.

Children

JRVIS AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
